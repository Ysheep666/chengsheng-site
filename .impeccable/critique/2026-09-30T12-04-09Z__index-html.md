## Priority Issues

### [P2] Under 52rem the sticky download never appears
- **Why it matters**: At 320×700 the in-flow button starts at 814. A reader in the install or question bands has to scroll back to the hero or on to the close. The dock stayed hidden in 15/15 samples at 390 and 16/16 at 320 because the anchor list covers the page.
- **Fix**: Hide the dock only while `#download`, `#proof`, or the closing download button is on screen. Let it show across the legend, the local band, install, and questions. Keep it hidden at 320 scroll 0 so it does not cover 「待合成」.
- **Suggested command**: /impeccable layout

### [P2] Tabbing the sticky nav jumps about 460px
- **Why it matters**: At scrollY 1223, Tab from 「安装」 to 「问题」 landed at 763, and Shift+Tab landed at 303. Both links were already on screen. `.bar-download` restores scroll on focus; the nav links do not.
- **Fix**: On Tab, remember scrollY and restore it when focus lands inside `.bar`.
- **Suggested command**: /impeccable harden

### [P2] The nav goes blank between its targets
- **Why it matters**: At the legend (scrollY 640) and the local band (scrollY 962) all four links had no aria-current. The observer's root band misses the sections that are not themselves nav targets.
- **Fix**: Mark the last section whose top has passed the reading line.
- **Suggested command**: /impeccable clarify

### [P3] The deck orphans 「句。」
- **Why it matters**: The second line breaks as 「…只重跑这一」 / 「句。」
- **Fix**: Keep 「这一句。」 on one line.
- **Suggested command**: /impeccable typeset

### [P3] A bad checksum has no action beside the sentence
- **Why it matters**: Recovery says 「重新下载 zip」 but the only zip controls are the hero and the close.
- **Fix**: Make that phrase the zip download, and say the Terminal line must match the hash underneath character for character.
- **Suggested command**: /impeccable clarify
